# ECONOMICS.md

## 1. Overview

This document estimates the unit economics of the AI Spend Audit tool as a lead-generation product for Credex.

The goal is not direct monetization from users, but conversion into high-value Credex customers through identified AI spend inefficiencies.

---

## 2. Value of a Converted Lead

A qualified lead is a startup that:

- Has $200–$2000/month AI tooling spend
- Identifies at least 20–40% potential savings
- Is actively scaling engineering or product teams

### Estimated value:

- Average Credex customer savings opportunity: $500–$1500/month
- Credex capture rate (commission/credit margin): ~10–20%
- Monthly value per converted customer: **$50–$300**
- Annual value per customer: **$600–$3600**

👉 Conservative average: **$1200/year per converted lead**

---

## 3. Funnel Assumptions

### Step 1: Audit completion → Lead capture
- Conversion rate: 40–60%
- Reason: users only give email after seeing savings value

---

### Step 2: High-savings detection → Credex interest
- Conversion rate: 15–25%
- Trigger: savings > $200/month shown clearly

---

### Step 3: Credex consultation booking
- Conversion rate: 20–35% of high-savings users

---

### Overall funnel estimate:

100 users →
- 50 audits completed
- 15 high-savings users
- 5–7 consultation bookings
- 2–3 paying customers

---

## 4. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

### Channels from GTM plan:

#### 1. Direct outreach (X / LinkedIn)
- Cost: $0
- Time cost: high
- CAC: $0–$5

#### 2. Reddit / Indie Hackers
- Cost: $0
- CAC: near zero

#### 3. Organic sharing (viral URLs)
- Cost: $0
- CAC: $0

👉 Weighted CAC: **$1–$8 per lead**

---

## 5. Revenue Potential (Credex angle)

If scaled:

### Scenario: 10,000 audits/month

- 5,000 leads captured
- 500 high-savings users
- 100–150 Credex conversions

At $1200/year per conversion:

👉 Annual revenue impact: **$120K–$180K/month equivalent pipeline value**

---

## 6. Conditions for $1M ARR

To reach $1M ARR (~$83K/month):

We need:

- ~70–100 paying Credex customers/month
- Conversion rate from audit → customer: ~1–2%
- Monthly audit volume: ~8,000–12,000 users
- High-savings detection accuracy must remain strong (>20%)

---

## 7. Key Risks

- Incorrect pricing assumptions could reduce trust
- Low perceived savings reduces conversion rate
- Over-reliance on AI tool trend volatility
- Users may self-optimize without purchasing Credex credits

---

## 8. Key Insight

This product is a **top-of-funnel intelligence layer**, not a standalone SaaS.

Its economic value comes from:
- identifying inefficiency
- creating urgency
- routing users into Credex ecosystem
